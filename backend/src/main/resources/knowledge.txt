### COLLEGE RULES
1. College ID Card is mandatory at all times on campus and during events.
2. Discipline & decorum must be maintained throughout all fests and competitions.
3. No damage to college property will be tolerated under any circumstances.
4. Consumption of alcohol, smoking, or any illicit substances is strictly prohibited on campus.
5. Outside visitors are only allowed with valid guest passes or official invitations.
6. Ragging is strictly prohibited by law; zero-tolerance policy applies with immediate suspension.
7. Event registrations must be completed before the specified deadline.
8. Professional behavior is expected from all participants, organizers, and audience members.

### REGISTRATION PROCESS
1. Login to the student portal using your student USN and password.
2. Browse through available clubs and events on the Dashboard or Clubs section.
3. Click on the 'Register Now' button for the desired event.
4. For group events, create your team, specify team name, and add team member USNs.
5. You will receive an instant confirmation upon successful registration.
6. You can view all your registered events anytime in the 'My Participation' tab.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How can I register for an event?
A: Log in to your student account, go to the Events page, select your event, and click Register. Follow the prompts to register individually or create/join a team.

Q: Can I participate in multiple events?
A: Yes, you can register for multiple events as long as their timings do not clash with each other.

Q: What happens if I miss the registration deadline?
A: Registrations strictly close at the specified end time. Late registrations are not accepted under any circumstances.

Q: Who can participate in events?
A: All currently enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students with a valid college ID card are eligible to participate.

Q: Can I cancel my registration?
A: Yes, you can cancel your registration from the 'My Participation' section prior to the registration close date.

Q: How are winners announced?
A: Event winners are evaluated by event judges and published on the Results & Winners leaderboard by the organizing club admin.

Q: How can I see my registered events?
A: You can view your registered events by clicking on 'My Participation' in the left navigation sidebar or by visiting your Profile section.

### SSP SCHOLARSHIP (State Scholarship Portal - Karnataka)
The State Scholarship Portal (SSP) is a unified platform by the Government of Karnataka to provide pre-matric and post-matric financial assistance to eligible students.

Eligibility:
- Karnataka domicile students.
- Belongs to SC / ST / OBC / Minorities / EWS categories.
- Family annual income within the prescribed government limits (typically under 2.5 LPA for SC/ST, under 1 LPA to 2 LPA for OBC).
- Enrolled in recognized post-matric professional or non-professional courses.

How to Apply:
1. Visit the official Karnataka SSP Portal (ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in).
2. Create an account using your Aadhaar number and mobile number.
3. Enter your SATS ID / Student USN and college details.
4. Upload required documents (Caste/Income certificate RD numbers).
5. Submit the application and download the acknowledgment receipt.
6. Submit a hard copy of the acknowledgment along with document photocopies to the college scholarship desk.

### NSP SCHOLARSHIP (National Scholarship Portal)
The National Scholarship Portal (NSP) is a one-stop digital platform by the Government of India that facilitates various central and state government scholarship schemes.

Major Schemes under NSP:
- Post Matric Scholarships Scheme for Minorities (Ministry of Minority Affairs).
- Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships for College and University Students.
- Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme for Central Armed Police Forces & Assam Rifles.
- Post-matric Scholarship for Students with Disabilities.

Eligibility:
- Minimum 50% to 80% marks in the previous final examination (depending on the specific scheme).
- Annual family income limit as specified by the respective scheme (typically under 2 LPA to 8 LPA).
- Student must have an Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

How to Apply:
1. Go to scholarships.gov.in.
2. Click on 'New Registration' and complete Aadhaar OTP eKYC.
3. Login using Application ID and password.
4. Select the applicable scheme and fill in academic & personal details.
5. Upload necessary certificates (income certificate, fee receipt, marks cards).
6. Final submit and submit the printed application copy to the college office.

### SCHOLARSHIP DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Aadhaar Card
- Income Certificate (RD Number)
- Caste Certificate (if applicable)
- Previous Year Marks Card
- Bank Passbook (Aadhaar linked)
- Passport-size photo
- Fee Receipt
- Bonafide / Study Certificate
- SATS ID / Student ID (for SSP)

### SCHOLARSHIP TIPS
- Bank account must be Aadhaar-linked
- Details must match exactly (Aadhaar, marks card)
- Don't wait until last date (server issues)
- Check status regularly on portal
- If rejected, correct details and reapply
